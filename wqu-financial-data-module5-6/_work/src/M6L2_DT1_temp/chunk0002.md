   Technology
   Innovations in artificial intelligence, climate models, and cloud computing are improving the ways
   users work with Earth science data, especially massive datasets like those expected from NISAR.
   NASA leverages modern computing approaches to optimize the quality of data collected and the
   speed at which users are able to drill down to the details they need to support on-the-ground
   science.
