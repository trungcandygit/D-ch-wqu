                                                                   In this lesson, we examine the characteristics of critical
                                                                   thinking. We'll look at a systematic literature review that
                                                                   addresses how critical thinking skills were used to determine if

                                                                   1. Critical Thinking
                                                                   Critical thinking is not just something we’ll discuss once. As
                                                                   Ismail writes, “Critical thinking involves the purposeful, goal-
                                                                   directed thinking during the process of making decisions based
                                                                   on the evidence in the scientific problem-solving process" (1).
                                                                   Throughout this program, you'll be expected to utilize critical
                                                                   thinking skills for a variety of purposes, but here, we'll focus on
                                                                   using critical thinking to determine whether a source is reliable.

                                                                   The article by Ismail addresses the importance of critical
                                                                   thinking skills for getting hired and being productive once
                                                                   employed. The article highlights a study that was done on
                                                                   employees who had five years of experience in an engineering
                                                                   job, particularly mechanical engineering. The period of five
                                                                   years is considered to be a starting point for building maturity
                                                                   in one's career.
