In producing high potential mechanical engineering students to fulfil the gap of the current job market,
the emphasis should be givens on the elements of critical thinking and problem-solving skills. The item
has become an interest and is no longer a requirement. Students in this area are advised not to only rely
on their academic textbooks alone as this will limit their level of creative thinking. So, instructors and
academician must also act as facilitators in guiding and practising self-reflection processes which are
essential elements in critical thinking and abandon chalk and talk teaching techniques. Research also
proves this element of soft skills needed by the industry towards a positive and high qualified engineer.
Mechanical Engineering environment is circulating and moving forward according to the rapid
technological advancement, so it is not too much to say, graduates and engineers of Mechanical
Engineering need to pay more attention in applying and exploring critical thinking in their future career
endeavours. Therefore, competence in soft-skill is main criteria required by the industry in hiring skilled
proficiency employees and holistic engineers.
